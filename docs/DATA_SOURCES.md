# Data Sources and Citations

This document lists every source used to build the project database (`data/health_monitor.sqlite`). It also states which data is real and which is synthetic.

**Scope of the database:** body and cabin temperature, cabin pressure and blood pressure, cardiovascular function, oxygen saturation (SpO₂) and space radiation.

---

## 1. Declarations

1. **Synthetic data.** All crew and cabin time series (`crew_member`, `measurement`, `daily_measurement`, `event`) are **synthetic**. They were generated by `data/scripts/generate_synthetic.py` and calibrated with the reference values cited in section 3. They do not describe any real astronaut, mission or spacecraft.
2. **Real data.** The tables prefixed `nasa_` contain **real, public metadata and published results** from the NASA Life Sciences Data Archive (section 2). They are used as evidence and traceability, not as patient data.
3. **No personal data.** The database contains no names, ages, birth dates, photographs or identifiers of real people. Crew members are pseudonymous codes (`CREW-LEO-01`). Privacy-protected NASA human data, available only through a formal data request, was **not** requested or used.
4. **Project heuristics.** Thresholds whose source is `SRC_PROJECT` are design choices of this project, not validated clinical values. They are: the radiation spike ratio, the heart rate limit during exercise, the cabin pressure band, and the critical limits for cabin temperature.
5. **Not a medical device.** This is an educational hackathon prototype. Its thresholds must not be used for clinical decisions.

## 2. Real datasets: NASA Life Sciences Data Archive (LSDA)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Archive | NASA Life Sciences Data Archive (LSDA), Johnson Space Center and Ames Research Center |
| Access portal | NASA Life Sciences Portal (NLSP): https://nlsp.nasa.gov/explore/data |
| Starting file | `by_topic` CSV export of the LSDA dataset catalog (13 files: `datasets_all.csv`, `datasets_human.csv` and 11 per-topic files). Columns: `dataset_catalog`, `dataset_name`, `health_topics`, `species`, `experiment_id`, `experiment_title`, `mission_id`, `experiment_type`, `availability`, `processing_level`, `parameters`, `file_name`, `file_format`, `file_size`, `release_date`, `description`, `nlsp_url` |
| Dataset records | Public JSON records of the NLSP portal, `GET https://nlsp.nasa.gov/api/v1/data/lsda_dataset/{id}`. All 2,390 records of the cleaned catalog were read (all responses HTTP 200) |
| Experiment records | `GET https://nlsp.nasa.gov/api/v1/data/experiments?page=1&limit=10000` returned 2,676 experiments with objectives, approach and results |
| Date accessed | 14 November 2026 |
| Not accessed | Raw data files (`.xls`, `.tif`, `.pdf`). The API does not expose their download keys. No privacy-protected data was requested |

### What is stored from LSDA

| Table | Rows | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `nasa_dataset` | 737 | Datasets related to temperature, pressure, cardiovascular, SpO₂ or radiation, flagged `is_public`, `is_aggregated` and `is_machine_readable`; each row keeps its `nlsp_url` |
| `nasa_experiment` | 207 | Experiments linked to those datasets, with objectives, approach and published results |
| `nasa_evidence_finding` | 268 | Sentences from the results sections, linked to a monitor metric |
| `nasa_dataset_metric_link` | 764 | Dataset ↔ metric links |
| `nasa_dataset_topic`, `nasa_dataset_mission`, `nasa_dataset_experiment` | — | Many-to-many relations |

**How to cite an LSDA record:** use the experiment or dataset title, its identifier and the `nlsp_url` stored in the database, followed by:

> NASA Life Sciences Data Archive. *[Dataset title]*. NASA Life Sciences Portal. [nlsp_url]. Accessed 14 November 2026.

### LSDA studies used to calibrate the reference values

| Source ID | Study | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `LSDA_DSO402` | DSO 402 (Space Shuttle): supine heart rate 57 bpm, rising to 70–71 bpm standing before flight; higher supine blood pressure after flight | Heart rate and blood pressure |
| `LSDA_DSO603` | DSO 603: about 20 % of astronauts at or near their cardiovascular limit after landing | Orthostatic response after a change of gravity |
| `LSDA_DOSIMETRY` | DSO 200 (STS-60, STS-63) and Mir 18 dosimetry: dose rates and peaks when crossing the South Atlantic Anomaly | Radiation dose rate and spike detection |

## 4. Verification of the citations

All citations and values were checked on **14 November 2026**:

| Check | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|
| DOIs (5) | Crossref API (`api.crossref.org/works/{doi}`) and PubMed E-utilities | All resolve to the cited article. Corrected: full title of the ACC/AHA guideline and issue number (6) |
| Radiation values | Abstracts and papers that report the values | Mars transit 1.84 ± 0.30 mSv/day, Mars surface 0.64 ± 0.12 mSv/day, Moon 1369 µSv/day, natural background 2.4 mSv/year, ISS 80–160 mSv per 6 months: confirmed |
| NASA-STD-3001 | Text of Vol. 1 Rev. C and Vol. 2 Rev. F | 600 mSv, 250 and 500 mGy-Eq confirmed. Corrected: cabin temperature 18.3–26.7 °C → **18–27 °C**. Clarified: the 34.5–103 kPa pressure range is a tolerance range, so our 1-atm leak band is now labelled a project heuristic. The 30-day and 1-year limits are in mGy-Eq (organ dose); the database compares them with mSv as an approximation |
| Clinical thresholds | NEWS2 chart, Freeman 2011, ACSM, ACC/AHA 2017 | All bands match (NEWS2 pulse, SpO₂, temperature and systolic bands; orthostatic ≥20/10 mmHg and ≥30 bpm; exercise SBP >250 / DBP >115; BP categories and >180/120 crisis) |
| NASA LSDA studies | Records stored in `nasa_evidence_finding` | DSO 402 (57 → 70–71 bpm), DSO 603 (about 20 %) and DSO 200 dosimetry match the archived text |
| Links | HTTP check | Updated: NEWS2 (new RCP address), NASA-STD-3001 (old address returned 404), ISS dose (specific NASA document). acsm.org blocks automated requests (HTTP 403) but exists |
